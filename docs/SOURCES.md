# Source and reproduction notes

## The historical document

- **The Conscience of a Hacker**, Loyd Blankenship (The Mentor).
- Written January 8, 1986. Phrack Volume One, Issue 7, Phile 3 of 10.
- Authoritative text: https://phrack.org/issues/7/hackers-manifesto
- The complete `<pre>` text was captured on November 5, 2026 as UTF-8,
  including its original attribution, signature, punctuation and line breaks.
- SHA-256: `f89a1f57a4f38b746a32f464ae680d5f9ba225941f14d46d65aef85e59a071cc`.
- No historical wording is edited. The mobile drawing rewraps lines only.

Phrack's published **Copynow Disclaimer Information** permits reproduction of
part or all of an article when Phrack and the author receive credit. The
published notice is preserved in the ChromiumOS Portage license collection:

https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromiumos/overlays/portage/+/326df9fd24a8099ea73a9988a1255683aff03490/licenses/phrack

Phrack retains its rights; the article is not represented as public domain.

owner appear only on the desktop wallpaper, outside the historical document,
and are not attributed to The Mentor. Both text files contain the original only.

## Wallpaper and artwork

Palette and background layering were inspected directly in
`johngrimmreaper/miranda-corp-www`, `src/styles/global.css`, November 5, 2026.
The paper palette is `#e7e2d4`, `#f3efe4`, `#d8d1bf`; ink is `#20231d` and
`#383c32`; olive, brass and line are `#696650`, `#c8b991`, `#b8b4a5`.
The SVG repeats the site's diagonal paper gradient, soft radial light,
corner shading, vignette and restrained CRT scanlines, with a technical grid.

NULL/X, its emblem, application frames and other desktop icons are original
vector drawings made for this profile. The Netscape icon uses the exact
1024 × 1024 PNG supplied by the profile owner on November 5, 2026: the gold
ship's wheel over its diagonal blue-and-black background. The full source is
preserved in `assets/icons/netscape-reference.png` with SHA-256
`4720594dfba04a5a013d126871c7a9722b704df106a4996a9fed1e2331fa36d0`.
The icon is downsampled to 128 × 128 with Lanczos resampling and displayed at
70 × 70 SVG units, without redrawing, cropping, recoloring or adding a letter.
The PNG bytes are embedded to avoid external resources inside the SVG.
